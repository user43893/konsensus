# Code Of Conduct

Konsensus contributors are expected to keep discussions careful, evidence-based, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be precise about claims and generous about misunderstandings.
- Critique ideas, code, methodology, and sources without personal attacks.
- Treat legal, political, identity, and privacy topics with extra care.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and editorial standards.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or sustained personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without clear consent.
- Posting credentials, identity numbers, private documents, secrets, or private correspondence.
- Deliberately misrepresenting sources, legal positions, or contributor intent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; and temporarily or permanently block contributors whose behavior puts the project or participants at risk.

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private maintainer or security
channel.
